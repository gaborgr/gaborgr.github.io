About Me

My journey into programming started in the corporate world. I worked in audit and finance, eventually becoming a Controller, which taught me how to read businesses from the inside out. Today, I bring that same perspective to software: before writing any code, I need to understand the business model, who the user is, and the actual value we're delivering.

I'm currently working as a Backend Developer on a production Django platform, while also building and launching my own web and mobile apps. I value teamwork and transparency. When I come across something I don't know, I just ask or figure out how to learn it. I'm not afraid to make decisions, and I welcome constructive feedback, because at the end of the day, I care more about getting things right than being right.
